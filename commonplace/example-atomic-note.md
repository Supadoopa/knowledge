# Spaced Repetition Compounds Learning

The act of reviewing material at increasing intervals produces retention that grows non-linearly with time invested. A single review session hours after first exposure is worth more than three reviews immediately after.

## Why this matters

Most note-taking systems optimize for capture, not retrieval. The value of a note is not in writing it but in re-encountering it at the right moment.

## Evidence / Examples

- Ebbinghaus forgetting curve: retention drops sharply within 24 hours without review
- Anki SM-2 algorithm: intervals double with each successful recall
