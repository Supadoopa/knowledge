Exam: Week 9

English — Persuasive Speech

Argue that Jack is NOT guilty of stealing from the Giant in Jack and the Beanstalk.

1. Introduction

  • Hook: Start with a bold statement or rhetorical question
  • State your position clearly: "Jack is not guilty."
  • Preview your three main arguments

2. Body Paragraph 1

  • The Giant stole Jack's family's wealth first
  • Jack's father's belongings were taken by the Giant
  • Jack was simply reclaiming what was rightfully his

3. Body Paragraph 2

  • Jack acted out of desperation to save his family from poverty
  • His mother and he were starving — a matter of survival
  • The law recognises necessity as a defence

4. Body Paragraph 3

  • The Giant was violent, dangerous, and threatened Jack's life
  • "Fee fi fo fum" — the Giant intended to harm Jack
  • Jack acted in self-defence

5. Conclusion

  • Restate your position strongly
  • Summarise your three arguments
  • Finish with a powerful call to action or statement
